## 3. Recommended Upgrades (all free / permissive)

The placeholder art is stylized-but-serviceable. For a fully **realistic** look,
drop in any of the following CC0/royalty-free sources (none are bundled —
download and import yourself):

| Asset type | Recommended source | License |
|---|---|---|
| PBR material sets (concrete, metal, gravel, rust, asphalt…) | [ambientCG](https://ambientcg.com) | **CC0 1.0** |
| PBR materials, HDRIs, models | [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com) | **CC0 1.0** |
| Rigged human characters (run/jump/slide anims) | [Mixamo](https://www.mixamo.com) (Adobe account) | **Free for commercial use** (Adobe General Terms — characters/animations may not be redistributed standalone) |
| Sound effects (footsteps, horns, impacts) | [Freesound](https://freesound.org) filtered by **CC0** | Per-clip (use CC0 filter) |
| Ambient city / rain beds | [Freesound](https://freesound.org), [Pixabay](https://pixabay.com) | CC0 / Pixabay Content License |
| Fonts (optional UI upgrade) | [Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com) e.g. Inter, Roboto | **OFL 1.1** |

### Mixamo notes
- Download FBX (with skin), Rig → *Humanoid*, then assign to
  `CharacterDefinition.modelPrefab` + `animatorController`.
- Parameter contract is documented at the top of
  `Assets/Scripts/Player/PlayerAnimatorDriver.cs`.
- Mixamo assets are free to use in games, but you may not redistribute the
  character/animation files as a standalone asset pack.
